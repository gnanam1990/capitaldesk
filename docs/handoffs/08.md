## Public contract changes

- Every active mandate binds a selected symbol, exact asset identities, per-plan and daily
  limits, a rational concentration bound, all four required freshness classes, plan lifetime,
  buy inhibit, risk halt and fee-policy version.
- Every strategy has its own target, per-plan debit and daily BUY limits. Target acceptance now
  loads that immutable strategy row instead of using the pool maximum as strategy authority.
- BUY budget use equals committed fills plus worst-case outstanding holds. Consumed amounts
  remain in the original UTC bucket; a zero-consumption settlement releases the hold.
- Publication is owner-only, optimistic-concurrency protected and idempotent. It invalidates
  unmarked plans through the existing restoration boundary; marked attempts remain in flight.
- Wire objects reject unknown fields. Monetary inputs remain canonical atom strings and policy
  decisions use bigint or exact rational comparisons.

## Failure scenarios encoded

- Venue surplus cannot replace an insufficient strategy-owned claim.
- Missing or stale price, account, symbol-metadata or venue-clock evidence fails closed.
- A stale policy/fee version, wrong symbol or asset, expired plan, owner halt, active buy
  inhibit, UTC rollover, daily-budget exhaustion and unprovable concentration are denied with
  stable reason codes.
- Same-pool concurrent holds are serialized under the pool lock so only capacity-backed holds
  can commit.
- Policy rows and strategy limits are immutable; budget holds permit only HELD to CONSUMED or
  RELEASED and are never deleted.
