visible. Preserve machine evidence separately; video is presentation evidence.

1. Readiness: testnet mode, stable-account digest, epoch, symbol, source freshness and fee
   capability.
2. Opening baseline and explicit HOUSE-to-strategy claims.
3. Two opposing targets entering `CONFLICT`, with zero venue request.
4. Revised compatible targets, exact FIFO, limits, fees, reservations and owner approval.
5. One actual `LIMIT IOC` dispatch and source order/trade/commission result.
6. Gross child completion beside net target satisfaction and any residual.
7. Dropped-response run: one downstream request, `UNKNOWN`, restart, exact-identity lookup and
   reconciliation without resend.
8. An external drift fixture labelled deterministic, followed by quarantine and the recovery
   explanation. Do not present it as a live external transfer.
9. Completed and unresolved exports, independent atom calculation and passing proof manifest.

Capture 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 widths, keyboard focus, denied actions, long identifiers and
degraded dependencies. Redact credentials and signed transport material before recording.
