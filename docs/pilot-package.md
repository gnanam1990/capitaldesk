# Bounded operator pilot

The pilot is for an operator already running at least two strategies that overlap one Spot
account and can describe a conflict, shared-capital workaround or recovery incident. It is a
testnet-only evaluation for one selected symbol. It makes no return claim and moves to funded
trading only under a separate owner decision after a passing release gate.

Measure:

- conflicting proposals stopped before a venue request;
- duplicate downstream requests during response loss;
- time to identify and resolve an uncertain attempt;
- unattributed or unconserved asset atoms;
- reconciliation time and unnecessary quarantine duration.

Interview questions:

1. Which strategies share balances today, and why are subaccounts insufficient?
2. Show the last conflicting order, oversubscription or ambiguous response incident.
3. Who decides allocation and approval, and what evidence is required afterward?
4. What delay or false block is unacceptable?
5. Would a separate subaccount, one-strategy-per-asset rule or manual sequencing solve this
   more cheaply?
6. What written acceptance result would justify a paid pilot?

Pricing remains a hypothesis: a fixed operator subscription or platform integration licence.
Seek three qualified interviews, one reproduced incident and one written paid-pilot intent.
Repository attention, prize availability and technical completion are not demand evidence.
No outreach, publication, registration or submission is performed by this package.
